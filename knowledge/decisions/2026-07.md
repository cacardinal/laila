## 2026-07-21 — Consolidate all recurring bills onto the shared checking account

**Decision:** Move the five recurring bills currently split across two personal cards onto the shared household checking account, and track them in a single `domains/household/tracking/recurring.md` table.

**Reasoning:** Two bills were missed in June because each card's autopay was checked separately and neither review caught both. One account means one review surface, and the monthly finance check can diff a single statement against the recurring table. The alternative (better reminders for two cards) treats the symptom; consolidation removes the split-attention failure mode entirely.

**Impact:** All five autopays migrated 2026-07-21; recurring.md created and is now the canonical bill list. Monthly finance review checklist updated to diff against it.

**Revisit:** At the October finance review — confirm zero missed bills across Aug/Sep, otherwise add a mid-month sweep.
